## 3. Analysis & Takeaways

### Architecture diversity

| Group | Models | Inductive bias |
|-------|--------|----------------|
| TSM (temporal shift) | `tsm`, `tsm_v2`, `tsm_v2_rot` | Shift-based temporal mixing inside every ResNet block |
| VFL (video former) | `vfl`, `vfl_focal`, `vfl_rot` | Cross-attention over T=4 frame tokens |

The two families have complementary inductive biases — local channel shift vs. global
attention over frame tokens — which is why a TSM+VFL ensemble outperforms either family
alone. The optimum is a 3-model log-softmax average: `tsm_v2_tta + tsm_v2_rot_tta + vfl_rot_tta`.
Adding the weaker closed models (`tsm_tta`, `vfl_tta`, `vfl_focal_tta`) slightly hurts, as
their noisier predictions dilute the two strong TSM signals (`log_avg` of all 6 = 0.4273).

### Why log-softmax averaging wins

Averaging in log-probability space (rather than raw logits or probabilities) down-weights
over-confident wrong predictions and rewards agreement, which suits an ensemble of models
with different calibration (rotating-fold vs. single-split, focal vs. CE).

---

## Important Caveats

1. **Val-dir selection bias:** the subset/strategy search is run directly on the 6,745-clip
   val-dir set, so the top configuration is slightly optimistic; true test-set performance
   may be 1–2 pp lower.
2. **TTA cost:** the best configuration uses 10-crop TTA (10× inference). Without TTA the best
   closed-track result is `log_avg(tsm_v2 + tsm_v2_rot + vfl_rot)` at approximately 0.41–0.42.

## Recommended Configuration

| Use case | Configuration | Expected top-1 |
|----------|---------------|:--------------:|

| Closed track, no TTA | `log_avg(tsm_v2 + tsm_v2_rot + vfl_rot)` | ~0.42 |
